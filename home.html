Commissions

If you’re interested in commissioning a painting, please don’t hesitate to get in touch!

This is how it works – get in touch with me via email to share the idea or image that you’d like painted and the canvas size you require. I can then inform you on the pricing and timescale for the piece. I always work collaboratively and will send an update once I’ve made good progress to give you a chance to share what you think of the piece so far. Once the piece is finished, it will be packaged securely and sent to you to enjoy!!

To note: I don’t currently offer framing services at this time.
